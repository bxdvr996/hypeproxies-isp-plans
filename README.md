## A compliant way to evaluate proxy infrastructure

If you have a legitimate business reason to test a proxy provider, run a small, documented evaluation. Keep it focused on your own systems and permitted activity.

1. **Define the approved task.** Write down what the connection will be used for, who owns the Marketplace asset or data involved, and what authorization exists.

2. **Confirm the required geography.** Use only a location that accurately reflects an approved business operation or test scenario. Do not use a proxy to misstate your identity, address, or Marketplace eligibility.

3. **Assign access deliberately.** Give each approved user or environment a documented endpoint when a dedicated connection is necessary. Avoid sharing credentials casually in chat channels.

4. **Test normal browser behavior.** Measure connection reliability, page-load consistency, and whether your approved tools work correctly. Do not test by collecting platform data at scale or trying to trigger and defeat safeguards.

5. **Review cost after one billing cycle.** Count the IPs that were genuinely required. If half of the pool sat unused, reduce the commitment rather than treating unused capacity as a badge of honor.

6. **Keep a fallback path.** Make sure the business can still access and manage its legitimate assets through its normal, authorized connection if the proxy service has an outage or maintenance window.
